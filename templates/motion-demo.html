01 — Reveal · the workhorse

Everything translates. Nothing fades in place.

translate 22px up (or 28px from left / right) · duration 420ms · curve cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1) · trigger 5% visible, bottom margin pulled in 12% · once

data-reveal · up 22px
data-reveal="left" · 28px
data-reveal="right" · 28px

Scroll away and back — they do not re-run. Reveals fire once, then unobserve.

02 — Stagger

A group cascades. A crowd does not.

step 70ms · cap 8 items for groups, 6 for siblings · set via --reveal-delay on each child, not baked into the component

The cap is the point. Without it a 30-card grid takes two seconds to finish and reads as slow rather than choreographed.

05 — Scrub sequence · desktop ≥56rem only

The scroll position is the easing.

trigger top 82% · end +=90% · scrub 0.6 · ease none · pin false · translate-only at 34px · stagger 0.35 · desktop only

Three lines, each translating 34px with a 0.35 stagger.

No opacity is animated — a scrub tween renders its start state at progress 0, so fading would leave this invisible if the trigger never fired.

Translate-only means a dead trigger costs a 34px offset and nothing else.

ease: "none" is deliberate. Adding a curve on top of the scroll curve makes the motion fight your hand.

06 — Collapsible

Collapse, do not stack.

The single biggest mobile fix. The stories index was 24.5 screens; collapsing to nine and filtering took it to 4.1.

Panel content. Real content, not padding — a fixed-height panel with dead space reads as unfinished.

Two or three short paragraphs. Keep prose inside 50–75 characters per line.

07 — Accent discipline · not an animation, but the same idea

The green is a signal, not a highlight.

It appears on exactly three things: the word "borders" in the hero, the globe arcs, and the primary CTA. A fourth use and it stops meaning go.
